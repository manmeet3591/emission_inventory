Labelling guide — Delhi SkySat pilot (0.5 m)

Golden rules. (1) Only label what you can see. If unsure, choose unsure or skip — a wrong label hurts more than a missing one. (2) Submit chips with nothing in them as empty — that is useful data. (3) Work in the prelabel_v0 tab so candidate boxes are already there; fix or delete them. (4) One chip ≈ 512 × 512 m. A car is ~8 px, a truck ~20 px, a kiln ~150–250 px long.

1. Brick kiln (brick_kiln, rotated box) — the main class

How to recognise one — look for at least 3 of these:

  • Shape: a compact reddish/brown rectangle or rounded oval, roughly 80–150 m long and 25–50 m wide, standing alone in farmland or at a village edge.
  • Colour: red/orange/pink (fired brick and ash) — stands out against green or brown fields.
  • Chimney: a single tall stack, often seen as a long thin dark shadow pointing away from the sun, from the middle or one end of the kiln.
  • Around it: rows of thin white/light lines (green bricks drying), dug-up bare earth (clay pits), stacks of finished bricks, a few worker huts.

Not a kiln: sports grounds/running tracks (oval but green or bare, no chimney, no brick yard), factories with metal roofs (grey/blue, regular rooflines), water tanks, farm sheds.

Box: fit the box tightly around the kiln body (the brick structure), not the whole yard. Rotate to match its long side (press R or drag the rotate handle).

kiln_type: fcbtk_oval = rounded/oval ends, like a running track. zigzag = sharp rectangular corners. circular_cfcbk = a round ring with the chimney in the middle (common around Lucknow). clamp = irregular heap of bricks with no permanent structure or chimney. If you can't tell: unsure. (Seed boxes came from a Sentinel-2 dataset; their kiln types are not verified — judge from the image.)

state: active_plume = visible smoke; active_no_plume = no smoke but fresh red colour and drying rows nearby; idle = intact but no drying rows, pale/faded; abandoned = broken down, overgrown or partly demolished; otherwise unsure.

2. Landfill (landfill, polygon)

Huge (hundreds of metres) mounds of dark grey/brown/multicoloured waste with terraces, curving vehicle tracks and trucks on top, in the middle of the city. Trace the outline of the waste mound by clicking points. Mark smoke on it as smoke_plume (box) and set state active_plume.

3. Vehicles and aircraft (rotated boxes)

truck: rectangle ~2.5 × 8–16 m (~5 × 20–30 px), often in rows at depots, markets, highways. bus: ~2.5 × 12 m, usually uniform colour, in depots. car: only label cars if the chip is a parking lot or depot — do not label every car on every road. aircraft: obvious; box the whole plane including wings.

4. Crematorium — label only when sure

At 0.5 m a crematorium is hard to spot without context. Clues: a walled compound (often by a river, canal or at a town edge) containing rows of small open-sided sheds or raised platforms (pyre bays, usually tin roofs), dark ash/soot patches, stacks of firewood in a yard, sometimes a single short chimney (electric/CNG furnace). Draw a crematorium_site polygon around the compound and cremation_pyre boxes on visibly burning or ash-covered platforms. Don't search for them — label only if you know one is there (e.g. Nigambodh Ghat on the Yamuna bank in the 2 Jun 2021 scene); we will seed known crematoria from maps later.

5. Other classes (draw if clearly present, otherwise skip)

  • construction_site (polygon): bare graded earth, foundations, cranes, rebar, sand/cement piles inside a boundary.
  • aggregate_stockpile (polygon): large sand/gravel/coal heaps. stone_crusher (box): conveyor + crusher + grey dust heaps.
  • chimney_stack (box): industrial stacks — box the shadow+base; cooling_tower, storage_tank: round, obvious.
  • open_burning (polygon): black burn marks or smoke in fields/garbage heaps; crop_burn_scar: blackened whole fields (Oct–Nov).
  • industrial_unit (polygon): factory compounds (metal roofs, loading yards). dairy_colony/cattle_shed: rows of long sheds with cattle and dung heaps.
  • bus_depot, rail_yard (polygons): obvious from parked buses / many parallel tracks.

Speed tip: aim for ~1 minute per chip. Kilns first (scenes 20220424 and 20201218), then landfill scenes, then the rest.
